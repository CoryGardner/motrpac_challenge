The Explorer

Pick a sample, set α and read the prediction set: confident, ambiguous or abstaining. Then follow each panel gene across MoTrPAC, the rat BodyMap and GTEx, build the panel gene by gene, and price a guarantee in animals.

The tissue card

Pick a sample and ask the fingerprint what it is; the set of tissues it returns is the answer it is willing to guarantee: one tissue is a confident call, several is ambiguity, none is abstention. Try a BodyMap thymus or uterus, organs the fingerprint has never seen, and a GTEx heart.

The gene explorer

One gene across the three datasets: log2 CPM by tissue in MoTrPAC (sex as marker shape), by organ and age in the rat BodyMap, and log2 TPM by tissue in GTEx through its 1:1 ortholog. Start with Pgk2, the testis marker not yet expressed in two-week-old rats, and Gnb3, the heart marker that points at the ovary in human.

Score your own samples

The scoring tool has moved to its own page, Check samples. It takes a 20-gene table or a full count matrix and adds claimed-label checks, a reference map and a per-sample explanation.

The panel builder

The round-robin selector takes the best remaining gene of each tissue in turn. Slide k along the evaluated grid and watch which tissues have a marker yet: at k = 10 only the first ten tissues in class order do, which is why accuracy jumps between 10 and 15.

The animals-needed calculator

A certificate that a panel makes at most α errors, with confidence 1 − δ, needs zero calibration errors on n ≥ ln δ / ln(1 − α) calibration animals: the guarantee is paid for in animals, not genes.
