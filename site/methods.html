Methods

Every number on this site was produced by the pipeline in scripts/ under the frozen rules in docs/EVALUATION_RULES.md, then exported to site/data/ with a provenance record. Nothing is typed by hand.

The pipeline, all of it inside the fold

Each outer fold holds out whole animals. Everything that learns from data — median imputation, the variance prefilter, z-scoring, the panel selector and the classifier — is fitted on the training animals of that fold only, as one scikit-learn Pipeline, so leakage is impossible by construction rather than by care.

The one documented deviation: the transcript gene filter (≥ 10 counts in ≥ 20 % of the samples of at least one tissue) runs on all samples before the split. It uses no labels and no test-fold statistics beyond presence, but it is not inside the fold; see Limitations.

Animal-grouped splits

The unit is the animal (pid). Five outer folds are stratified on tissue and grouped on animal (StratifiedGroupKFold), so all 18 vials of an animal are on one side of every split: 40 training and 10 test animals per fold. An assertion checks every split for leaked animals, and the test suite fails if a plain KFold over vials is substituted. Shift experiments are deterministic splits: leave one sex out, hold out one training time point, fit on sedentary controls only.

The selector, and why the F-test fails on 19 classes

The round-robin selector scores every gene for every tissue by a one-vs-rest effect size, |mean in − mean out| / pooled sd, then takes the best remaining gene of each tissue in turn until k genes are chosen. A univariate F-test ranks genes by a single statistic dominated by the tissues with the largest effects, so its top 20 are markers of the same few easy tissues and most tissues are left without one. The Fingerprint page shows the two curves.

Conformal prediction sets and the quantile rule

Split conformal. Inside each fold the training animals are divided again into fit animals (18) and calibration animals (22); test animals are never used for either. The nonconformity score of a calibration vial is 1 − p̂(true tissue) (LAC). The threshold is

q̂ = the ⌈(n + 1)(1 − α)⌉-th smallest of the n calibration scores,
    and +∞ (every tissue in every set) when that rank exceeds n.

A tissue enters a test sample's set when p̂(tissue) ≥ 1 − q̂. With one vial per animal, n = 22, and at α = 0.10 the rank is 21 of 22; at α = 0.05 the rank is 22, the largest score; with n ≤ 18 the rank exceeds n and the set is full. Mondrian sets use one threshold per true class from that class's calibration scores, falling back to the marginal threshold for a class with no score; the floored variant never lets a class threshold fall below the marginal one, so its sets contain both the marginal and the Mondrian sets. Pooled-vial calibration uses all vials of the calibration animals (n ≈ 396); it sits closer to 1 − α but treats vials of one animal as exchangeable, which they are not.

The certificate

“What is the smallest panel whose error is at most α with confidence 1 − δ?” is a multiple-testing problem over panel sizes. For each k on the grid the k-panel is fit on the fit animals and its errors are counted on the calibration animals (one vial per animal); a one-sided Clopper–Pearson upper bound on the error rate is compared with α; fixed-sequence testing walks from the largest k down and stops at the first failure. With n = 22 a panel with zero errors is certified only when the bound ⌈…⌉ allows it: n ≥ ln δ / ln(1 − α) calibration animals with zero errors are needed (59 at α = δ = 0.05, 22 at 0.10/0.10). The certificate is therefore a distribution over repeated fit/calibration splits, reported as such.

The transfer protocol

Rat BodyMap (GSE53960, 316 samples, 11 organs, 4 ages) and GTEx v8 (2,485 samples, 862 donors, 17 tissues, ≤ 150 donors per tissue) are scored with panels fit on all 50 MoTrPAC animals: the same imputation and prefilter, every gene z-scored within its own dataset, the classifier's coefficients unchanged. Muscle and brain are super-classes (any of their rat tissues counts); thymus and uterus have no MoTrPAC tissue and are out-of-distribution; Artery – Aorta → vena cava is an imperfect mapping. GTEx uses 1:1 orthologs only (14,569 of 21,193 genes), and its panel is re-selected in that ortholog space. For conformal transfer the models are refit on 35 MoTrPAC animals and calibrated on the other 15; recalibration draws 3 or 5 target individuals (20 draws; all combinations where fewer) and tests on the rest. Every BodyMap split is grouped on the assumed animal, every GTEx split on the donor.

The frozen evaluation rules and the self-check scheme

The rules are in docs/EVALUATION_RULES.md in the repository (frozen; the repository link is in the footer). Two self-contained notebooks, 01_replication.ipynb and 02_transfer.ipynb, paste the library in verbatim, recompute the headline numbers and compare them with notebooks/expected_values.csv (288 keys; the file records both the pre-fix published value and the post-fix reference value with its tolerance). A drift check inside each notebook fails if the inline library differs from src/. The site adds a third layer: tests/test_site_data.py checks every provenance entry against its results file, and tests/test_site_conformal.js checks the browser's conformal port against Python-computed sets, including infinite thresholds.
