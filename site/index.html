Tissue identity check

Is this sample the tissue you think it is?

Drop in an expression table and get back, for every sample, a tissue call, its 90 % prediction set (the tissues the model cannot rule out), the genes behind the call, and a flag when your label doesn't fit.

How it works

  1. Drop in an expression table. CSV or TSV, the 20-gene panel or a full raw-count matrix.
  2. Get a call, a 90 % prediction set and the genes behind it, with flags for swapped labels, contamination and unknown tissue.
  3. Samples from another lab: expect many “Unknown” at first; recalibrate on at least nine of your own labelled samples.

A conformal prediction set lists the tissues the model cannot rule out. On samples like its held-out calibration animals it contains the true tissue at least 90 % of the time; that 90 % describes the sets, not the confidence of any single call. One tissue is a confident call, several is ambiguous, none means the model abstains.

What you can upload

  • Rat bulk RNA-seq.
  • CSV or TSV with a header row.
  • Format A: the 20 panel genes in log2 CPM, samples in rows and genes as columns; or format B: a full raw-count matrix, genes × samples or samples × genes, Ensembl IDs or gene symbols (converted to log2 CPM here with the pipeline's transform).
  • If no tissue clears the calibrated threshold, the sample is marked Unknown: the model abstains.

Optional · flags swaps

A claimed_tissue column (or tissue, true_tissue) with the tissue you think each sample is; it is compared with the call.

Use the tissue names in the list of accepted names (the 19 reference tissues and their synonyms, e.g. brain, muscle, fat).
