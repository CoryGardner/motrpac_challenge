The five-minute tour

  1. Open the Explorer and pick a BodyMap thymus sample: an organ the fingerprint has never seen. Watch it abstain (an empty set) instead of guessing, then move the α slider.
  2. Still in the Explorer, pick the GTEx heart sample it opens on, with the 20-gene panel, and read the two calls on the card: the panel fit on all 50 animals, the one the accuracy tables use, calls this human heart skeletal muscle; the 35-animal refit that carries the guarantee may not. Switch to the full model and compare.
  3. Open the gene explorer on Pgk2: the testis marker is absent in 2-week-old rats (no spermatids yet), which is why juvenile testes are mislabelled.
  4. On the Transfer page, look at the recalibration-cost scatter: three BodyMap animals repair the guarantee at one tissue per set; three GTEx donors do not.
  5. On the Identifiability page, read the nesting heatmap: every RNA plate, library batch and flowcell holds whole tissues, so within-study accuracy cannot separate biology from processing. The evidence is the transfer.
